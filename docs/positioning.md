# Positioning, audiences and content strategy

## One-line position

Forward deployed and applied AI engineer who builds agentic systems for business operations with human approval gates, and who brings sixteen years of running the operations those systems automate.

Everything on the site exists to make that sentence credible to three readers.

## Ideal readers

| Reader | What they need in 30 seconds | Where the site answers |
|---|---|---|
| Hiring manager or recruiter for FDE / Applied AI roles (AI labs, AI-native startups, enterprise AI teams) | Evidence of shipped, maintained software; a clear role title; a one-page résumé; signs of judgement about safety and reliability | Hero proof strip, `/projects/sahkarmi`, `/resume`, `/writing/what-a-forward-deployed-ai-engineer-does` |
| Founder or operations lead at a small or mid-size business in India considering agents | Does this person understand my operations, not just the model? What would an engagement look like? What are the risks? | `/services`, `/about` (sixteen years timeline), `/writing/revenue-operations-taught-me-to-distrust-automation`, contact form intents |
| Engineer or technical evaluator | Is the code real? How is it built? Is the site itself competent? | Every project links to its repository; `/ask` explains its own retrieval; the site repo is public; CI and tests are visible |

## Differentiators that are true

1. Sahkarmi: fifty tagged releases, Microsoft Store listing, Scoop bucket, about 650 unit tests, approval-gated agent runtime. Few applied-AI candidates have a shipped desktop product with a release pipeline.
2. Sixteen years of enterprise revenue operations (Star Cement, Shyam Steel, MP Birla Cement). Domain fluency in dealer networks, rebates, collections and MIS.
3. Honesty as a feature: statuses, limitations and verification dates on every project. Recruiters at AI labs are trained to discount portfolio inflation.

## What was deliberately not claimed

Production architectures counts, latency figures, client names and revenue impact without a public artefact. The site says "prototype" where it is a prototype.

Each piece must contain something countable or runnable.

1. Sahkarmi permission policy deep-dive with the sixteen categories and the six hard gates, linking to the source files.

5. Monthly "verified facts" update to `profile.ts` so the `verifiedOn` date on the hero stays current.
