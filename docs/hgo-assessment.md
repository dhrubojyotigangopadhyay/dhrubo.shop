# "HGO" assessment

The brief asked for "HGO" alongside SEO, AEO and GEO and asked that it not be invented if it is not real.

## Finding

Searches on 2 November 2026 across Google, Bing, vendor documentation (Google Search Central, Bing Webmaster, OpenAI, Anthropic, Perplexity) and marketing-industry sources found no established optimisation methodology called HGO. The only consistent hits are:

- "Human Group Optimizer", a metaheuristic algorithm in optimisation research (unrelated to web content).
- Isolated agency blog posts coining "Human Guided Optimization" or "Hybrid Generative Optimization" with no shared definition, no tooling and no adoption.

HGO is therefore treated as not an established discipline and no "HGO strategy" is claimed on the site.

## What was done instead

The intent behind the term, as far as it can be read, is "optimise for the human reader in an AI-mediated search world". The established frameworks that cover that are Google's people-first content guidance and E-E-A-T (experience, expertise, authoritativeness, trust), plus the accessibility standard WCAG 2.1 AA. The rebuild applies them:

- Experience: first-person articles about systems the author built, with links to the code.
- Expertise: a technical explanation of the site's own retrieval, and limitations stated on every project.
- Authoritativeness: consistent entity (name, handle, photo, role) across the site, JSON-LD, GitHub, LinkedIn and the Sahkarmi store listing.
- Trust: verification dates, no unverifiable numbers, privacy page, consent banner, public source.
- Human readability: WCAG AA contrast, reduced-motion support, text-first rendering, 16 px body type, 68-character measure.

If a reader of the brief had a specific definition of HGO in mind, it should be supplied and this assessment revisited.
